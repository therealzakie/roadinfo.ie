Part 1. The Troubles

During The Troubles, Northern Ireland had closed their borders. That included the Aghalane Bridge going over the Woodford River. The bridge crossed between County Cavan and County Fermanagh, making it a link toward Enniskillen, well, up until The Troubles occoured. The bridge was targetted by Loyalists paramilitaries and finally left impassible at 1973. The fastest/safest work-around was through the and the . It wouldn't be after 1999 (which was a year after The Troubles ended) where the bridge was replaced by a new bridge named after USA Senator George Mitchell, and was built beside the old Aghalane Bridge.
